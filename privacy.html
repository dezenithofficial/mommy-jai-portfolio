YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This notice describes how collaboration inquiries sent through the Mommy Jai Ferrer creator portfolio are handled.

Information collected

When you submit a collaboration inquiry, we collect your name, brand or business name, email address, collaboration type, campaign message, and confirmation of consent. We do not ask for payment details or passwords.

Why we collect it

We use these details to review your proposal, evaluate whether a collaboration is a good fit, and respond to you about your inquiry. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Where it goes

Until the online form service is activated, the inquiry form prepares an email draft that you must send through your email application. Email messages go to mommyjaiferrer@gmail.com and are handled by the sender's and recipient's email providers. Once the hosted form is enabled, submissions will be processed by Formspree for email notification and submission history. The website itself does not maintain a separate inquiry database.

Access and retention

Inquiry emails are accessible to the operator of Mommy Jai Ferrer's collaboration inbox. They are kept only as reasonably necessary to manage collaboration correspondence and records. The form service applies its own retention policies, and messages in the recipient inbox remain until they are removed.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you submitted by contacting mommyjaiferrer@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify your inquiry before acting on a request.

Security and contact

We use HTTPS connections for the website and form submission. No online service can guarantee absolute security. For privacy-related questions, contact mommyjaiferrer@gmail.com.
